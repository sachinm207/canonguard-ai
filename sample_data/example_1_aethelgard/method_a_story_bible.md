# STORY BIBLE: Aethelgard: The Broken Crowns
Genre: Grimdark Fantasy / High Magic
Era: 1240–1325 (Aethelgard High Annals)
Setting: The Seven Shattered Realms of Aethelgard

## Dramatis Personae (Character Canon)
- Character: High King Valerius IV (Born: 1242, Died: 1298, Status: Dead) - The Iron Sovereign of Oakhaven. Assassinated during the 1298 Winter Solstice feast.
- Character: Lady Morwen of House Karst (Born: 1265, Died: 1340, Status: Alive) - Arch-Mage of the Obsidian Spire and master of protective wards.
- Character: Lord Commander Branok (Born: 1250, Died: 1294, Status: Dead) - Blood Shield of Karst. Executed after the siege of Red Mire in 1294.
- Character: Prince Kaelen the Frozen (Born: 1270, Stasis: 1292-1320, Status: Stasis) - Heir to Frostpeak. Sealed in cryo-enchanted amber stasis from 1292 to 1320.
- Character: Seraphina the Blind Prophet (Born: 1220, Died: 1289, Status: Dead) - Seer of the Weeping Mire whose visions foretold the shattering of royal relics.
- Character: Grand Inquisitor Malakor (Born: 1258, Died: 1318, Status: Alive) - Ruthless keeper of the Red Ledger and high law enforcer.

## Relic and Artifact Continuity
- Relic: Sun-Forged Blade of Eldor (Forged: 1180, Shattered in: 1285, Status: Destroyed) - Sacred ancestral broadsword of the High Kings. Vaporized into molten slag during the Cataclysm of Blood Ridge in 1285.
- Relic: Crown of the Seven Wyrms (Forged: 1210, Melted in: 1299, Status: Destroyed) - Golden circlet melted in dragonfire during the fall of Highkeep in 1299.
- Relic: Eye of the Void Orb (Created: 1235, Destroyed in: 1294, Status: Destroyed) - Obsidian sphere used to channel necromantic ley lines. Crushed to dust by Branok in 1294.
- Relic: Amulet of the First Archon (Forged: 1150, Status: Active) - Preserved safely in the vaults of the Obsidian Spire.

## Universal Axioms & Physical Invariants
- Rule: Any spellcaster channeling raw blood sorcery without an inscribed silver ward ring suffers instantaneous arterial combustion.
- Rule: Frost Drakes cannot breathe combustion flame and perish within three minutes if exposed to desert heat exceeding 40 degrees Celsius.
- Rule: No sovereign bearing the Curse of the Weeping Mark may touch the Iron Sept scepter without disintegrating into obsidian ash.
