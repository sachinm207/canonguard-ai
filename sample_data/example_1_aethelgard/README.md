## Canon Rules & Invariants To Observe
1. **The Sun-Forged Blade of Eldor:** Shattered into molten slag in **1285 A.D.** during the Cataclysm of Blood Ridge. Any mention of this sword being brandished or possessed after 1285 is a **Critical Causal Paradox**.
2. **High King Valerius IV:** Slain by poison in **1298 A.D.** He cannot appear alive in any scene set in 1310 A.D.
3. **Prince Kaelen the Frozen:** Placed in cryo-enchanted amber stasis from **1292 A.D. to 1320 A.D.** He cannot walk or fight in 1310 A.D.
