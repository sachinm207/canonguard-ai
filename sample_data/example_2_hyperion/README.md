## Canon Rules & Invariants To Observe
1. **Hyperion Dark-Matter Drive:** Vaporized in **2175 A.D.** during the Ganymede Breach. Trying to spool or activate it in 2182 A.D. is a **Critical Causal Paradox**.
2. **Admiral Teresa Cruz:** Died in **2172 A.D.** during the Phobos Station hull breach. Cannot command bridge operations in 2182 A.D.
3. **Special Agent Gabriel Cross:** Under suspended animation in stasis pod 09 from **2165 A.D. to 2190 A.D.** Cannot be awake or drawing weapons on the bridge in 2182 A.D.
