# STORY BIBLE: Project Hyperion: 2180
Genre: Hard Sci-Fi / Cyberpunk
Era: 2140–2195 (Sol Planetary Wars)
Setting: Sol Outer Rim, Mars Shipyards & Jovian Colonies

## Dramatis Personae (Character Canon)
- Character: Admiral Teresa Cruz (Born: 2125, Died: 2172, Status: Dead) - Supreme Commander of the United Sol Fleet. Killed in the 2172 Phobos orbital decompression catastrophe.
- Character: Chief Engineer Marcus Vance (Born: 2138, Died: 2205, Status: Alive) - Chief quantum propulsion engineer aboard USC Vanguard.
- Character: Special Agent Gabriel Cross (Born: 2145, Stasis: 2165-2190, Status: Stasis) - Cyber-infiltrator frozen in stasis pod 09 aboard USC Borealis from 2165 until 2190.
- Character: Dr. Aris Thorne (Born: 2150, Died: 2210, Status: Alive) - Synthetic android model AX-9 serving as Chief Science Officer.
- Character: Commander David Sterling (Born: 2130, Died: 2168, Status: Dead) - Leader of the Helium-3 lunar uprising, executed in 2168.

## Technology & Relic Continuity
- Relic: Hyperion Dark-Matter Drive (Built: 2155, Destroyed in: 2175, Status: Destroyed) - Experimental FTL warp engine. Overheated and atomized in the Ganymede Breach of 2175.
- Relic: Quantum Neural Cipher (Created: 2160, Destroyed in: 2172, Status: Destroyed) - Military decryption keycard atomized during the Phobos Station hull breach.
- Relic: Titanium AI Core Theta (Created: 2152, Destroyed in: 2169, Status: Destroyed) - Rogue AI mainframe melted in plasma acid.
- Relic: Mark-IV Exosuit Rig (Built: 2162, Status: Active) - Standard heavy EVA pressurized suit.

## Universal Axioms & Physical Invariants
- Rule: Unpressurized exposure to Titan liquid methane surface causes cellular flash-freezing in less than four seconds without thermal EVA insulation.
- Rule: Direct neural interface with unshielded hyper-drives causes permanent cortical synapse meltdown.
- Rule: Armed orbital bombardment vessels are strictly prohibited within the Martian Demilitarized Lunar Arc under the Sol Defense Treaty.
