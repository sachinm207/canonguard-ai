📖 CanonGuard AI: Simple Guide for Everyone

Like Grammarly or Spellcheck, but for 50 years of movie & TV storylines!

🌟 1. What is CanonGuard AI? (The 10-Second Summary)

Think of CanonGuard AI as "Grammarly for Movies." When you write a script for big franchises like Marvel, Star Wars, or Lord of the Rings, it's impossible to remember every rule and character from 50 movies. If you accidentally write something that breaks past history (like a dead hero showing up, or using an ancient sword that was melted 3 movies ago), CanonGuard AI instantly draws a red squiggly line under the mistake and gives you 3 creative ways to fix it in 1 click!

💡 Why it saves millions: If a continuity mistake isn't caught until after filming, movie studios have to spend $10M to $30M on emergency actor reshoots and CGI fixes. CanonGuard AI catches it right at your keyboard!

🖥️ 2. What Everything on Screen Means

  • 🌌 Universe Dropdown: Choose your story world! Switch between The ChronoVerse (Sci-Fi Spy Thriller), Galactic Imperium (Space Battles), or Mythos Realm (Fantasy with Knights & Magic).
  • Trap 1 / Trap 2 / Trap 3: Test buttons! Click any trap to paste a famous movie continuity mistake into your script and watch the AI catch it in less than 1 millisecond.
  • Cold War Requiem.fountain: The file name of your script. .fountain is the free standard format screenwriters use to type scripts without expensive software.
  • Setting & Scene Year (1982): The "where" and "when" of your scene. The AI needs to know the year so it knows who is alive, who is frozen in ice, and what weapons exist at that time.
  • 📂 Upload Script: Upload any script from your computer or just drag-and-drop a file directly onto the editor.
  • 📥 Upload Lore Bible: Want to test your own book or game world? Click this to upload your custom characters, rules, and history!
  • 💾 Save / Export: Downloads your active script as an industry-standard .fountain file. You can open it in Final Draft, Highland 2, or Fade In without any proprietary lock-in.
  • Auto-Save: All your typing is continuously auto-saved to your browser storage, so your draft is never lost even if you refresh.
  • Auto-Patch: Click this 1-click button on the right panel to automatically fix the mistake in your script with the AI's canonical suggestion.

📚 3. Simple Glossary (No Technical Jargon)

Word Plain English Meaning
Canon The official, true facts of a story world. (If it happened in the official movies, it's "canon").
Retcon Short for "Retroactive Continuity". Accidentally or intentionally changing past facts (e.g. Bringing a dead character back without explanation).
Lore The history, character timelines, and world rules of a story universe.
Demo Trap An intentional test mistake to demonstrate that the system catches errors instantly.
Creative Mitigation Instead of just saying "Error!", the AI gives 3 friendly story ideas to fix the plot hole while keeping your scene exciting.
Sub-20ms Target Checking the rules faster than 20 milliseconds (0.02s). Our system checks in under 1 millisecond, so typing feels completely instant.
ClickHouse The ultra-fast database engine working behind the scenes to check 50 years of movie facts in a fraction of a millisecond.
