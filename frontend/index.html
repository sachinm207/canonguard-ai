Simple Guide: How to Use CanonGuard

A friendly 1-minute guide to checking your movie scripts for story mistakes

✨ What is CanonGuard? (In Simple Words)

Think of CanonGuard as a smart spellchecker for movie stories. When writing a script for a big series (like Marvel, Star Wars, or Harry Potter), it's easy to forget who died, who was frozen in ice, or what magical weapons were destroyed 3 movies ago. CanonGuard watches your typing in real time and warns you immediately if you write an impossible plot hole!

🚀 How to Use It in 3 Easy Steps

1

Type Your Scene

Write dialogue or action in the editor on the left, or click one of the Sample Mistakes buttons at the top to test.

2

See Red Underline

If an action contradicts a past movie, the system underlines the word in red and explains why it broke history.

3

1-Click Story Fix

Click "Apply Fix" on the right panel to automatically update your script with an approved story alternative!

🎯 Try the 3 Demo Buttons at the Top

⚡ 1. Frozen Hero:

Elena talks to Viktor in 1982, but Viktor was frozen in a cryo-pod from 1975 to 1995! CanonGuard warns you that Viktor cannot be in Berlin.

⚡ 2. Destroyed Item:

Elena pulls out the Sunstone crystal in 1982, but the stone was permanently melted in a reactor in 1960! CanonGuard flags the dead artifact.

⚡ 3. Broken World Rule:

Lord Vane removes his helmet on Planet Oberon, but the air is toxic ammonia! CanonGuard catches the broken physical rule.

📖 Common Words Meaning (No Confusing Jargon)

Word Used Plain English Meaning
Story Mistake (Retcon) An accidental plot hole that breaks past movie history (like bringing back a dead hero without explaining how).
Canon / Movie History The official, true facts established in past released films that writers cannot contradict.
Story World (Universe) The movie series you are writing for (e.g. ChronoVerse, Space Battles, or Fantasy Kingdom).
Story Bible The master encyclopedia listing all characters, important weapons/items, and physical universe rules.
Suggested Fix A creative story idea suggested by the AI that solves the plot hole while keeping your scene exciting.
Fast Database (< 1 ms) We use ClickHouse, an ultra-fast database, so checking 50 years of movie facts happens in less than a millisecond with zero lag.
